Emergency Response

24/7 Emergency Electrical Repair in Kentucky

Kentucky's spring storms and ice-heavy winters are hard on above-ground lines, panels, and outdoor equipment. When something goes wrong after hours, it usually falls into one of these categories:

Warning Signs That Need Immediate Attention

  • A burning smell near an outlet, switch, or the panel itself
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly with no obvious cause
  • Warm or discolored outlet covers and switch plates
  • Flickering lights across multiple rooms, not just one bulb
  • Sparking outlets or visible arcing at the panel
  • Total loss of power to part of the house after a storm

If you notice any of the above, shut off power to the affected circuit at the panel if it's safe to do so, and call a licensed electrician immediately. These aren't "wait until Monday" problems — they're the leading causes of residential electrical fires in the state.

Panel Upgrades

Electrical Panel Upgrades and Rewiring

A huge share of Kentucky's older housing stock — particularly in Louisville's historic neighborhoods and small towns across the state — is still running on 60 or 100 amp fuse panels never designed for today's air conditioners, electric ranges, and home electronics. Upgrading to a modern 200 amp breaker panel isn't just about convenience; it's about safety margin.

Signs Your Panel Needs an Upgrade

  • Your home still uses screw-in fuses instead of breakers
  • You rely on extension cords or power strips as permanent wiring
  • An insurance company has flagged the panel brand or age
  • You're adding a hot tub, EV charger, or workshop and need more capacity
  • The panel is rusted, humming, or shows scorch marks

We handle the permit, the utility coordination, and the final inspection so the upgrade goes smoothly from the first walkthrough to the final sign-off.
